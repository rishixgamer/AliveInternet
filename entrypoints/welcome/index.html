AliveInternet hides some rule matches and content you choose to mute. It works in your browser, starting with YouTube.

Stays on your device

No comment uploads, telemetry or remote service. Your preferences are saved locally.

You can undo every hide

Show an item temporarily, unhide an account or pause filtering. Nothing is deleted on the website.

Rules have limits

Spam can slip through. Legitimate content can be hidden. A rule match is never proof that someone is a bot.
