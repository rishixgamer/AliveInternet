AliveInternet 0.3 alpha

On-device, reversible social feed filtering. No content is sent to a server; a problem report opens only after you review it and click.

Hidden on this page

Review current local hides and reveal them temporarily. Excerpts stay in this extension and are never saved. “Report wrongly hidden” prepares a redacted draft you review before anything is opened.

Press Refresh to review the active page.

    Review a report before it leaves your browser

    Usernames, display names, @mentions, emails, phone numbers and page addresses are removed; links keep only their hostname. Quoted comment text can still identify its author if someone searches for it, so edit or delete anything you would not post publicly. Nothing is sent until you press the button below. It then opens a prefilled GitHub issue in a new tab (a public post under your GitHub account) that you still have to submit yourself. Nothing is stored by AliveInternet.

    What to filter

    This heuristic does not prove an account is a bot. It never adds accounts to your permanent blocklist.

    Personal mute words

    Applied to comments and posts on enabled sites. These are your preferences, not spam claims. Maximum 200 entries, 2–80 characters each.

    Never automatically hide these accounts

    Use the site and profile key, for example youtube:@creator or x:@creator. Each entry applies only to that site. Maximum 500 entries. Explicit local account hides still take priority.

    OSINT indicator advisories (opt-in)

    Matches come from a user-imported JSON pack. This is not automatic threat attribution and NEVER auto-hides an account or post.

    No pack loaded

    Websites

    Only YouTube is enabled by default. Other adapters are experimental, may miss content, and need real-browser verification.

    Locally hidden accounts (0)

    Hides matching visible accounts on this device. It does not press the platforms' own Block buttons or change server-side account settings.
